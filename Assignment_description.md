# Group 07  ·  Eyes closed, rhythm changed?

Can you see someone close their eyes in the frequency content of an EEG?
PhysioNet has separate eyes-open and eyes-closed baseline recordings for the same participant. Begin with one electrode near the back of the head and several short windows from each condition.

## Suggestions for exploring and analysing the data
1)  Select a participant and inspect the EEG for artefacts or interruptions.
2)  Study how the signal varies over time at one electrode.
3)  Explore which rhythms are present and whether they change between eyes-open and eyes-closed recordings.
4)  Compare the same rhythm across electrodes or participants.

## Techniques you could try
Estimate the power spectrum at one electrode and compare band power across eyes-open and eyes-closed windows.

You can also explore the data in a different way or pursue another analysis. It is up to you.
